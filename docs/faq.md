# TonoCard AI Phone Secretary Card: common questions

[简体中文](faq.zh-CN.md) · [Product overview](../README.md)

TonoCard is a Bluetooth hardware accessory and companion app for AI call handling on your existing phone. This page explains its connection method, controls, and usage conditions.

## Does TonoCard use carrier call forwarding?

No. TonoCard connects to your phone like a Bluetooth headset. The app connects to cloud AI, which handles the conversation. You do not need to enable carrier call forwarding, add a SIM card, or get a new phone number.

## Can I take over a call from AI?

Yes. You can take over the same call. Bluetooth disconnects and recording stops when you do. You cannot hand that same call back to AI after taking over.

## Can I use only the app?

No. The companion app requires TonoCard hardware. The phone must remain connected to Bluetooth and the internet, and the app must be running for AI call handling.

## Does it work with iOS and Android?

Supports iOS. Due to the wide range of Android devices and operating system versions, the experience with the standard app may vary on some devices. Check the current app instructions for the phone and software you intend to use.

## Is this just a call recorder?

TonoCard answers calls and responds to the caller in real time. Recording and summaries are supporting features for reviewing the part handled by AI. A recording device's ability to capture audio does not by itself provide AI call answering.

## Where are recordings and summaries stored?

You can review the AI-handled part locally. For the Overseas Edition, call records are temporarily retained in TonoCard's cloud for processing and delivery, then deleted from its cloud systems after successful client retrieval. Retrieved records remain on the client. Third-party processing and retention follow separate provisions in the [Overseas Edition privacy policy](https://privacy.tonocard.com/), sections 1.4, 2.2, and 4.1. Follow the policy applicable to your app's distribution edition. TonoCard uses cloud AI, so it is not a fully offline system. Recording stops when you take over.

## Can TonoCard answer calls in messaging apps?

The product supports voice-call handling in WeChat, WhatsApp, and LINE. Availability depends on the device, app and system versions, settings, connection, and service conditions; follow the companion app's current instructions.

## Can AI make a call for me?

TonoCard supports AI callbacks based on a stated call goal. The product also provides supported workflows for AI tools on a Mac or PC. See [agent calling](agent-calling.md). Available features and allowances depend on the current plan.

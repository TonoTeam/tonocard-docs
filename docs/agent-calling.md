# Calling from AI agents with TonoCard

[简体中文](agent-calling.zh-CN.md) · [Product overview](../README.md)

TonoCard AI Phone Secretary Card connects a phone call to AI through Bluetooth hardware, a companion app, and cloud processing. Supported workflows let AI tools on a Mac or PC help carry out a phone-call task using the owner's phone.

The product's public examples include **Workbuddy and Codex**, and tasks such as appointments, restaurant reservations, home-service bookings, repair requests, and business invitations. These examples do not imply support for every AI client or every client version.

## Where to start

1. Review the agent-calling scenario on the [official TonoCard website](https://www.tonocard.com/). Installation packages and client-specific instructions are supplied separately for supported integrations.
2. Set up TonoCard hardware and the companion phone app. Confirm the phone's compatibility and keep the required Bluetooth and internet connections available.
3. Follow the current installation and authorization instructions supplied with the supported integration.
4. Give the AI tool a specific contact and call goal, then follow the displayed confirmation flow.
5. Review what the call accomplished and any information that still needs your decision.

Use the current package's accompanying instructions for client configuration and authorization. This page is an overview of the publicly presented workflow.

## Describe a task the caller can act on

A useful call request identifies the intended recipient, the objective, relevant facts, and the outcome you need to know. For example:

> Call the restaurant at the number I provide and ask whether it has a table for four at 7 p.m. Report the available times before confirming a booking.

This is an illustrative request, not a report of a completed call. The actual workflow and confirmation steps depend on the supported integration and app version.

## What stays the same

The phone remains the calling device. TonoCard uses its Bluetooth connection and the app to connect the call with AI; the owner does not need to enable carrier call forwarding or obtain a new number. The hardware, phone, app, service, and applicable plan are still required.

Client compatibility and installation steps depend on the integration version. Check its current instructions and confirm your active entitlements in the app before starting a call.
